Yes. In fact, for a **hackathon**, this is the right approach.

Don't try to replicate S&P or SCB exactly. Build a **believable end-to-end flow** that demonstrates the concept. Judges care much more about the workflow and explainability than whether your ESG formula matches a commercial rating agency.

## Why this POC works

This demonstrates the entire business process:

```
Corporate Client
        │
        ▼
Uploads ESG Documents
        │
        ▼
AI Extracts ESG Evidence
        │
        ▼
AI Answers Bank ESG Questionnaire
        │
        ▼
AI Calculates ESG Score
        │
        ▼
AI Explains Missing Evidence
        │
        ▼
Green Finance Recommendation
        │
        ▼
ESG Analyst Reviews & Approves
```

This is a complete, realistic proof of concept for a green finance hackathon. It clearly shows how AI can reduce manual document review while keeping a human analyst in control of the final lending decision.

Once this flow is working, you can extend it with OCR, geospatial climate risk, regulatory alignment (ISSB/TNFD), or more sophisticated scoring without changing the overall architecture.
