## My suggestion for the next iteration

These are intentionally concise. For the hackathon, I recommend making them much more realistic by generating:

* **25–30 page Sustainability Reports** with:

  * Cover page
  * CEO message
  * ESG strategy
  * Climate disclosures
  * Emissions tables
  * Water and waste KPIs
  * Biodiversity section
  * Worker safety metrics
  * Governance
  * Appendix
* **40–60 page Annual Reports** with:

  * Company profile
  * Financial highlights
  * Board of Directors
  * Risk management
  * MD&A
  * Corporate governance
  * Sustainability references

These longer reports will give your AI document extraction pipeline realistic content to parse and make the demo feel much closer to a real banking workflow.
